About Pakistan's Tourism

Pakistan is a land of extraordinary diversity, home to some of the world's tallest mountain ranges, ancient civilisations, and vibrant cultural heritage. From the Karakoram Highway winding through snow-capped peaks to the bustling bazaars of historic cities, Pakistan offers a travel experience unlike any other.

The country is home to six of the world's seventeen peaks above 8,000 metres, hundreds of ancient archaeological sites, and a culinary tradition that spans thousands of years. Whether you seek adventure, history, or natural beauty, Pakistan has something extraordinary to offer.

Why Visit Pakistan?

Natural Wonders

Pakistan is home to K2, the world's second highest peak, along with Nanga Parbat, Broad Peak, and countless glaciers. The Karakoram, Himalayan, and Hindu Kush ranges converge here, making it a paradise for trekkers and mountaineers from around the world.

Rich History & Heritage

From the ancient ruins of Mohenjo-daro to the Mughal architecture of Lahore, Pakistan's history spans over 5,000 years. The country hosts numerous UNESCO World Heritage Sites and archaeological wonders that draw historians and explorers alike.

Warm Hospitality

Pakistanis are known worldwide for their warmth and hospitality. Guests are treated with great respect, and visitors consistently describe their welcome as one of the most memorable aspects of their journey through Pakistan.
