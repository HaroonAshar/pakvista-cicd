Introduction

Welcome to Karachi

Sitting on the shores of the Arabian Sea, Karachi is the capital of Sindh and the largest city in Pakistan, home to more than 16 million people. As the country's financial and industrial hub, it never sleeps — its streets pulse with commerce, its kitchens serve some of the finest food in South Asia, and its coastline draws crowds from dawn until long after dark.

A true melting pot, Karachi blends colonial-era architecture, modern skylines, bustling bazaars, and a seaside that defines daily life for its residents.

Top Attractions in Karachi

From the founder's marble mausoleum to the breezy shores of the Arabian Sea.

Quaid's Mausoleum (Mazar-e-Quaid)

The final resting place of Muhammad Ali Jinnah, the founder of Pakistan, this iconic white marble mausoleum was completed in 1971. Set within a large landscaped park, its gleaming domes and ceremonial guard make it the most recognisable landmark in the city.

Clifton Beach & Seaview

Karachi's most popular seaside escape, Clifton Beach stretches for kilometres along the Arabian Sea. Locals flock here for camel rides, street food, and sea breeze, making it the beating heart of the city's leisure life — especially at sunset.

Mohatta Palace & Bazaars

A short drive away, the elegant Mohatta Palace museum showcases the city's heritage, while the historic Empress Market and Saddar bazaars overflow with spices, textiles, and crafts — a sensory introduction to Karachi's trading soul.
