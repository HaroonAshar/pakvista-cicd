Hunza Valley – Mountain Paradise

A breathtaking destination in Gilgit-Baltistan, famous for snow-capped peaks, crystal-clear lakes, historic forts, and unforgettable landscapes.

Welcome to Hunza Valley

Hunza Valley is one of Pakistan's most spectacular tourist destinations, located in the northern region of Gilgit-Baltistan. Surrounded by the majestic Karakoram Mountains, Hunza is known for its natural beauty, peaceful atmosphere, and rich cultural heritage.

The valley attracts travelers, photographers, and adventure seekers from around the world. From historic forts to turquoise lakes and panoramic mountain viewpoints, Hunza offers experiences that leave lasting memories.

Top Attractions in Hunza

Rakaposhi Viewpoint

Rakaposhi Viewpoint offers stunning views of Rakaposhi, one of the most beautiful mountains in the Karakoram Range. Visitors can admire the snow-covered peak rising dramatically above the valley while enjoying the peaceful surroundings and fresh mountain air.

Attabad Lake

Attabad Lake was formed in 2010 after a major landslide blocked the Hunza River. Today it is famous for its striking turquoise water, scenic boat rides, and spectacular mountain backdrop, making it one of the most photographed locations in Pakistan.

Baltit Fort

Baltit Fort is a historic fort located in Karimabad and is more than 700 years old. The fort reflects the rich history and architecture of the region while offering panoramic views of Hunza Valley and the surrounding mountains.
